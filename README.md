## `01 // A LITTLE ABOUT ME`

Hey! I'm **Priyansha**, a third-year Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering student at Symbiosis Institute of Technology, Pune.

I like understanding how things work beneath the surface — from the behavior of electronic circuits and microcontroller interrupts to the software that transforms raw sensor readings into something useful.

My playground sits somewhere between **embedded systems, electronics, signal processing, and software development**.

🔌 I make sensors talk to microcontrollers.

📊 I turn signals and measurements into meaningful results.

🖥️ I build dashboards that make technical systems easier to understand.

🩺 I'm exploring how engineering can contribute to healthcare technology and medical-device security.

🏎️ I've also explored powertrain and electrical engineering through Formula Student.

I'm a quick learner, a hard worker, and someone who'd rather troubleshoot a stubborn bug than leave it unexplained. Give me a new tool, a challenging problem, and a little time to experiment — I'm in.
